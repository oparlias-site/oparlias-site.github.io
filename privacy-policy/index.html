Privacy Policy

Inuecs Oparlias Published on 11 September 2026

Overview

This website "Oparlias", available at oparlias.com is a static website. It is designed to respect your privacy completely. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is (and isn't) collected when you visit.

The short version: Nothing is collected. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking, no data harvesting. You are anonymous when you visit this site.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.

  • We do not use cookies or any similar tracking technologies.

  • We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

  • We do not collect, store, or process names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any other personal data.

  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties — because we don't have any data to share.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies.

Analytics and Tracking

This website does not use any analytics or tracking services. We have no way of knowing who you are, where you came from, or what pages you viewed.

Server Logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft), located in the United States.

When you visit this website, GitHub automatically records standard server access data, including your IP address, request timestamps, browser user agent, and pages accessed. This is done for security and infrastructure purposes, and is standard for virtually all web hosting services .

  • We do not access, use, or analyze these logs.

  • This data is processed by GitHub on US-based infrastructure and is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement: GitHub Privacy Statement .

  • No tracking cookies are set by GitHub Pages itself, and this logging does not include user tracking or marketing .

Note for EU/UK visitors: GitHub processes server access logs under the legal basis of legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for network security and infrastructure management. GitHub maintains Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework to safeguard international data transfers .

HTTPS and Security

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS, which means all communication between your browser and the site is encrypted. This protects your connection from being intercepted or tampered with in transit.

Third-Party Content

  • This website does not embed any third-party content, fonts, scripts, or resources.

Links to Other Websites

This site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Children's Privacy

This website does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You are anonymous to us by design.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Since the site collects no data, we don't anticipate meaningful changes — but we'll note them here if they occur.
